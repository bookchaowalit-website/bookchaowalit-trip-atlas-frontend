# Product: Trip Atlas — Route Planning Desk

## Product surface

Trip Atlas is a small, illustrative itinerary desk for choosing a route, balancing a day plan, and keeping a trip budget visible.

## Primary user

A solo traveler sketching a short trip before booking anything.

## Product promise

Turn a destination idea into a route with a next stop, not a list of disconnected links.

## Boundaries

- Use synthetic destinations and prices only.
- Do not imply live booking, map, or travel advisory data.
- The demo is a product design study, not a travel service.

## Design mode

operate

## Interaction contract

- Selecting a route changes the itinerary and budget readout.
- Selecting a day changes the focus stop.
- The next-stop control advances the route locally.
